Privacy Policy

At OpenSky Aviation, we recognize that our engineering data is the backbone of our safety standards. This policy explains how we manage and protect information within our internal portal.

Data Security & Encryption

All communication between your browser and our servers is encrypted. Access to specific engineering logs, schedules, and team configurations is strictly enforced based on your verified department credentials.

What We Collect

  • System Logs: We track login attempts to detect and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Operational Data: Technical logs and messages entered into the system for fleet maintenance records.
  • Profile Details: Essential information provided by your administrator to facilitate teamwork.

Data Sharing

Your data is never sold. Information is only shared internally across authorized departments to ensure operational efficiency and aviation safety compliance.
