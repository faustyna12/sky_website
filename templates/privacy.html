Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

1. Data Collection

Open Sky Aviation Group collects essential professional data including employee names, engineering credentials, department affiliations, and system access logs. This data is used exclusively for maintaining the Engineering Directory and ensuring operational security.

2. Purpose of Processing

Information processed within the Open Sky platform is used to facilitate team coordination, manage project dependencies, and provide secure authentication for the Engineering Map and Schedule tools.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Access to sensitive organizational maps is restricted based on the "Principle of Least Privilege" (PoLP).

4. Corporate Compliance

This policy aligns with global aviation data standards and internal Group Security protocols. For data deletion requests, please contact the IT Compliance Officer.
